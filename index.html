03 — Process

How a project runs

A clear plan up front, something you can install early, and no surprises at the end.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A short call to understand who the app is for, what it must do on day one, and what can wait.

    You get a summary of requirements and open questions

  2. 02

    Scope & quote

    A written plan broken into milestones, each with a deliverable, a price and a date.

    You get a fixed quote and timeline to approve

  3. 03

    Design

    Screens and flows agreed before the heavy engineering starts, so changes are cheap.

    You get a clickable prototype

  4. 04

    Build

    Work lands milestone by milestone, as real builds you install and try on your own machines.

    You get installable builds and weekly updates

  5. 05

    Launch & handover

    Final installers for every platform you need, published and documented.

    You get installers, source code and documentation

Every project includes

  • Installers for each platformSetup files and portable builds, tested on clean machines.
  • Full code ownershipThe source is yours, in your repository, from the first commit.
  • An update pathVersioned releases, so users upgrade without reinstalling.
  • DocumentationWritten so another developer could pick the project up.
  • Post-launch supportBug fixes after release, and ongoing support if you want it.
